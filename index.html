AI can now build a first version of almost anything. The harder question is what should be built, why, and who answers for it when it goes wrong.

Execution gets faster and cheaper every month. Critical thinking decides whether that speed takes you anywhere worth going. Experience Haus has built a framework for exactly this — three layers to work through before any AI product or service gets built — and uses it with financial and professional services teams in London and New York.

In this hands-on hour, forty people put it to work on real products. Experience Haus brings the framework and the facilitation. DDX brings the room.

Your morning

The thinking happens in the room.

Two hours, start to finish. Arrive and settle in, spend an hour hands-on with your team, then stay for brunch and the conversations that follow.

  1. 01.

    9:00 — 9:30 · 30 mins

    Arrive and settle in.

    Coffee on arrival and introductions before we start, so you know who is in the room before the work begins.

  2. 02.

    9:30 — 10:30 · 60 mins

    The workshop.

    We set the scene with real cases of AI going wrong, then split into three teams. Each takes a pre-briefed AI product through intent, intelligence and integrity, using prompts, guides and decision points, with a facilitator in every room. We finish together to share what each team found, and you leave with the full framework.

  3. 03.

    10:30 — 11:00 · 30 mins

    Brunch and networking.

    Time to carry on the conversation over brunch with forty leaders working through the same questions.

Who's running it

Built for the people doing the work.

Experience Haus is a design, innovation and AI capability-building consultancy.

For nearly a decade they have helped teams build the skills and habits to design better products and services, and today that means using AI with clarity, confidence and accountability. The framework you will use in this workshop is the same one they bring into client organisations — Bank of New York, DTCC, Spotify, Hiscox, Accenture.
